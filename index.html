How it works

One shared calendar. Two very different problems solved.

Offices set the rules once. Reps see only what they're allowed to book — nothing more.

01

The office sets access rules

Staff define visiting hours, frequency caps per rep or per company, and which specialties or vendors are welcome — then either post open slots or leave requests open for review.

02

A verified rep requests or claims a time

Reps browse only the offices they're credentialed for, request a specific slot, or grab an open one directly — no phone tag with the front desk.

03

The office confirms — or doesn't have to

Requests route to staff for a one-tap approval; claimed open slots confirm instantly within the office's own rules. Everyone works from the same ledger.

Verification

Every rep on the ledger has been checked.

Offices see a verified badge, not a promise.

01 — Company email

Domain verification

Reps sign up with a company email address, auto-matched against known pharma and device manufacturer domains.

02 — ID upload

Manual credential review

Independent reps or unrecognized domains upload a company ID or badge, reviewed by our team before verification is granted.

03 — One badge, every office

Portable verification

Once verified, a rep's badge carries across every office on Arrowhead — no re-checking credentials office to office.
